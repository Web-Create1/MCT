# Quick-Reference Card Outline

A quick-reference card (QRC) is what a crew member reads with the event in progress. It fits
one printed page and can be used cold by someone who missed the brief.

## Card Sections

Title line: `QRC: <trigger>`. Provenance line. BLUF: one sentence saying what this card is for.
Then:

1. **Trigger.** What the crew sees, with the threshold and unit.
2. **First three actions.** Numbered, each with a role code, each a verb first.
3. **Who to tell.** Role codes and the words to use.
4. **What to log.** The log kind and the fields that should appear, so the debrief can find it.
5. **When it happened.** Only if the debrief met this trigger: one line with `t_s` and `seq`,
   and the word simulated. Otherwise `Not met in this run`.

## Rules

- Actions match the brief exactly; the card never invents a new response.
- No more than three actions. A fourth means the trigger is two cards.
- No background, no theory. Those belong in the glossary.
